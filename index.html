How to read this

The works are the fact; the scores are not. Each of the three numbers was assigned by an AI at the maintainer's request — one pass, no panel, no appeal — which is exactly why they are printed rather than hidden. Form can be argued from the frame. Signal and Spread are predictions, and predictions are what benchmarks are for: come back in a year and see which works the numbers got wrong. Where an axis has nothing to measure it shows a dash rather than a zero; a zero is a judgement, a dash is an admission.

Every column is the arguable one

This is art. A machine is a poor sole judge of it, a market is a worse one, and the fix for both is the same: bring a score you disagree with and say why.
