Art should…

  • Make the invisible visible. Reveal hidden systems, forces, inner states.
  • Compress complexity. Turn complexity into a simple signal.
  • Generate emotion. Create a felt reaction.
  • Spark discussion. Open a loop. Start debate.
  • Force a reflection. Interrupt autopilot. Make you think.
  • Reprogram your brain. Shift your perspective.
  • Fill the room with energy. Amplify mood and momentum.
  • Inspire you to do something. Ignite action.
  • Be visually pleasing (or disturbing). Delight. Or unsettle.

Some effects are instant. Others unfold over time.
